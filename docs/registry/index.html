THE THIRD LEG OF THIS PROJECT'S MECHANISM IS NOT AUTOMATIC, and this is the most consequential thing found on 21 Aug 2026. 'The fund shall be blocked in the account till the time mandate is expired, revoked or the mandate amount is exhausted.' The rail KEEPS the unused remainder blocked. Neither circular imposes any duty to release it after a partial debit, and neither states any timeline for doing so. Release happens only because somebody calls revoke or update - see `merchant_revocable` and `customer_revocable`. Consequence for the ceiling model: debit Rs 470 against a Rs 620 block and walk away, and Rs 150 stays stranded until the customer-chosen end date, up to 90 days. THE ONE CONFLICTING SOURCE, and it should be disclosed rather than dropped.
